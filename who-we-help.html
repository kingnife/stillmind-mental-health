Tailored Lifespan Care

Care Across Every Stage of Life

Human challenges change across development. At Stillmind, our clinical team matches therapeutic pacing and tools to where you or your family member actually are.

Pediatric & Adolescent Practice In-person Indianapolis Office & Telehealth

Children & Young People

Ages 5 – 12

Supporting school-aged children navigating intense anxiety, ADHD, sensory overload, school refusal, and emotional meltdowns. We utilize play-informed, behavioral, and somatic regulation strategies.

Adolescents & Teens

Ages 13 – 18

A confidential, respectful space for teenagers confronting identity development, academic burnout, social anxiety, depressive withdrawal, and family conflict without patronizing lectures.

Parents & Caregivers

Family Consultation

Practical parent coaching, co-regulation techniques, and actionable advocacy tools for navigating Indiana school IEP/504 accommodation meetings and home routines.

Young Adults & College Students

Ages 18 – 25

Navigating independence, higher education pressures, early career turbulence, late-diagnosed neurodivergence (ADHD/autism spectrum), and establishing boundaries with family of origin.

Adults & Professionals

Ages 26 – 64

Evidence-based psychotherapy for career burnout, generalized anxiety, depressive cycles, relationship distress, parenthood overwhelm, and unresolved childhood experiences.

Older Adults & Seniors

Ages 65+

Gentle clinical care focused on life transitions, bereavement, chronic illness coping, retirement purpose, and sustaining psychological dignity and emotional clarity.
