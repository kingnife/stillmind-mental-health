Common Questions

Insurance & Billing FAQs

Can I use an HSA or FSA card?

Yes. Stillmind accepts Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Account (FSA) cards for all copays, deductibles, evaluations, and private-pay sessions.

How do out-of-network superbills work?

If we are not in-network with your specific insurer, we automatically generate detailed monthly superbills with standard diagnostic and CPT codes that you can submit for reimbursement.

Is telehealth covered identically to in-person?

Indiana state telehealth parity laws require commercial insurance carriers to cover telebehavioral health at the same benefit level as in-person clinic visits.

What is your appointment cancellation policy?

We require 24 hours advance notice for rescheduling or cancellations to allow waiting patients access to open clinical hours.
