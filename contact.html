Intake Guidance

Frequently Asked Contact Questions

Common questions regarding response times, records submission, and appointment booking.

How quickly will I receive a response?

Inquiries submitted during normal business hours (Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 6:30 PM) are typically answered within 4 to 8 business hours, and never longer than one full business day.

Can I book directly without sending a form?

Yes! You can bypass this inquiry form completely and book directly through our integrated Valant Patient Portal, which displays real-time clinician availability.

How do I submit previous medical or IEP records?

To protect HIPAA compliance, please do not attach records to this web form. Once you schedule an appointment, you will receive an encrypted upload link within the Valant portal or you may have your previous provider fax them to (317) 555-0189.

What should I do if I am in mental health crisis?

Stillmind Mental Health is an outpatient practice and cannot manage active psychiatric emergencies. If you are experiencing acute distress, call or text 988 immediately, call 911, or visit your nearest emergency room.
