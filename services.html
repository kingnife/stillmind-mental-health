Evidence-Based Modalities

How We Practice

We do not apply rigid cookie-cutter formulas. Our licensed therapists synthesize clinical modalities suited to your emotional and physiological needs.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

Identifying and reframing automatic negative thought loops, core cognitive distortions, and avoidance behaviors to reduce panic, rumination, and distress.

Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT)

Cultivating psychological flexibility, mindful awareness, and committed action aligned with your authentic values rather than struggle against difficult internal states.

Somatic & Nervous System Regulation

Grounding physiological hyperarousal, tuning into bodily warning signs, and calming the autonomic fight-flight-freeze responses to build lasting physical safety.

Collaborative Psychiatry & Primary Care

Close coordination between prescribing psychiatric clinicians and therapists to evaluate whether targeted medication can support therapy and quality of life.
