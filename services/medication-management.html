Our Prescribing Philosophy: Conservative and Collaborative

Medication is never a substitute for self-understanding, therapy, or healthy life rhythms; rather, it is a clinical tool used when biological symptoms (severe insomnia, unremitting panic, cognitive fog, vegetative depression) prevent psychological work from gaining traction.

At Stillmind Mental Health, our psychiatric providers operate on conservative, evidence-based principles: we prescribe the lowest effective therapeutic dosage, carefully assess side effect profiles, prioritize holistic metabolic health, and regularly evaluate whether medications can be safely tapered when stability has been sustained.

The Psychiatric Care Pathway

  • Comprehensive 60-Minute Initial Evaluation: Detailed medical history, lab work review (thyroid, metabolic markers), family genetic response, and nuanced symptom mapping.
  • Shared Medical Decision-Making: We explain mechanisms of action, anticipated timelines (e.g., 2–4 weeks for therapeutic onset), and common temporary side effects so you are an informed partner in your care.
  • Regular Monitoring & Dose Optimization: Follow-up sessions (25–30 minutes) scheduled every 2 to 4 weeks during titration, transitioning to quarterly maintenance visits once steady state is achieved.
  • Coordination with Your Primary Care Physician: We coordinate closely with your PCP or pediatrician to ensure systemic health and safety.
