Virtual Care Infrastructure

Indiana Statewide Telehealth

Stillmind is licensed to provide secure telebehavioral health to residents across Indiana, including Bloomington, Fort Wayne, South Bend, Evansville, West Lafayette, Carmel, and Terre Haute.

Sessions take place via high-definition, HIPAA-compliant video technology. There is zero software to install — patients receive a secure, one-click browser link prior to each appointment.

  • End-to-end encrypted audio and video stream
  • Accessible on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and PCs
  • Full insurance parity coverage across Indiana networks
